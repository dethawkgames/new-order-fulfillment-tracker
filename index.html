Pre-Shipment Check

Verify Asmodee Quote

Upload the Sales Quote your rep sends before anything ships, to catch a wrong SKU, wrong quantity, or missed item while it's still cheap to fix. This never changes anything — it only compares against the most recently locked Asmodee cycle (the Order placed, Shipped and Received tabs) and shows you the diff. It does not confirm shipment; that's the Invoice, below.

↑ Top

Upload Asmodee Invoice

Upload the Sales Invoice Asmodee sends once your order actually ships (not the Quote — that's a submission confirmation, not proof of shipment). Matching units move from Order placed to Shipped from supplier, oldest locked cycle first, and any order whose every unit has now shipped is tagged. Check "Dry run" to preview without writing anything.

Drop the invoice PDF here, or choose a file

Upload Asmodee Arrival Invoice

Upload the invoice confirming what physically arrived. Only units currently in Shipped from supplier move to Order received, matched against this invoice's line items by SKU — units awaiting a different invoice are left alone. Does not touch Shopify inventory. Check "Dry run" to preview the match without writing anything.

Upload ACDD Invoice(s)

Upload the ACD Distribution invoice PDF(s). Matching units move from Order placed to Shipped from supplier, oldest locked order first, and any order whose every unit has now shipped is tagged. Lines are matched by the UPC printed under each item (through the ACDD catalog), falling back to the product's Shopify barcode when a row has no ACDD SKU.
